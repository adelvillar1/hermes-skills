# Base Game Rules (reference)

Distilled from the 6th Ed rulebook "CATAN – The Game" (2025, CN3081) and the 5th Ed "Game Rules & Almanac" (2020). Edition differences at the end. Source: catan.com rulebook PDFs.

## Components (3–4 players)
- 19 terrain hexes: 3 hills (brick), 4 forests (wood), 4 pastures (wool), 4 fields (wheat), 4 mountains (ore), 1 desert
- 18 number discs: 5,6,8,9 each x2; 2,3,4,10,11,12 each x1 (the 2 and 12 are unique)
- Harbor/ports: 4 generic 3:1 + one 2:1 per resource (wood/brick/wool/wheat/ore)
- 95 resource cards (19 of each), 25 development cards, 2 special tiles (Longest Route/Road, Largest Army), 2 dice (red + yellow), 1 robber, 4 building-cost player aids
- Per color: 15 roads, 5 settlements, 4 cities

## Turn structure (6th Ed wording)
Two phases, in order:
1. **Production phase** — optionally play 1 development card (NOT one bought this turn; may be played before rolling) → roll 2d6, sum → producing hexes pay out → if 7, resolve the 7 (below).
2. **Action phase** — trade and/or build, as often as you like in any order, as long as you can pay. Then pass dice to the left.

Payout: settlement on a producing hex = 1 card; city = 2 cards. Multiple buildings stack. The robber's hex produces nothing. **Bank exhaustion:** if the supply can't cover everyone's production for a roll, NOBODY gets that resource — unless only one player is affected, then they get what remains.

## Resolving a 7 ("Activate the Robber")
1. Every player holding **more than 7** resource cards returns half (rounded down) to the supply. Dev cards are exempt and hidden.
2. The roller moves the robber to a DIFFERENT hex (the desert is legal; if the robber sits on the desert you must move it to a numbered hex).
3. Steal 1 random card (drawn blind) from a player with a building on the robbed hex; if several, the robber chooses whom to rob. Nobody on the hex = no theft.

## Trading
- **Domestic (with players):** only during your turn as the active proposer/acceptor; others may not trade among themselves or with the bank while you act. Announce what you want/give; counteroffers legal. No gifts, no even-for-even swaps of the same resource (3 ore for 1 ore is illegal), no dev cards.
- **Maritime (with the supply/bank):** 4 identical → 1 different of your choice, any time on your turn, from anywhere on the board.
- **Ports:** a building ON the port square enables 3:1 (any four alike) or 2:1 (two of the shown resource). You must actually occupy the harbor intersection.
- Between your turns you may only trade when it is another player's turn and they trade with you.

## Building
- **Road** (1 wood + 1 brick; 0 VP): on any empty edge connected to your own road/building network. Cannot start a road on the far side of an opponent's building.
- **Settlement** (1 wood + 1 brick + 1 wheat + 1 wool; 1 VP): empty intersection, connected to your road network, obeying the **Distance Rule** (at least 2 edges from EVERY building, yours or not). Max 5 (then upgrade to cities first).
- **City** (2 wheat + 3 ore; 2 VP): replaces one of your settlements only. Max 4.
- **Development card** (1 ore + 1 wool + 1 wheat): draw the top card; deck is face down; hand stays hidden. Never returns to supply; when the 25 run out, no more can be bought. Cannot be traded, given, discarded on a 7, or stolen by the robber.
- You may buy/build at any time during your Action phase, including your very first turn with your starting resources.

## Development cards (6th Ed names)
Deck composition: 14 Knight, 5 Victory Point, 2 Monopoly, 2 Road Building, 2 Invention.
- **Knight** (5th Ed: Soldier) — activate the robber (move + steal) without having rolled a 7 and without the discard step. First player to have PLAYED 3 keeps the Largest Army tile (2 VP); transfers immediately when someone plays more; on ties the current holder keeps it.
- **Victory Point** — 1 VP each, kept hidden; reveal only when declaring the win (or when someone else wins). Countable on the same turn you bought it.
- **Monopoly** — name one resource; every player gives you ALL copies they hold (then you rob no one; robber unaffected).
- **Road Building** — build 2 roads immediately at no cost. Each road must connect to your existing road/building network at the moment of building — the two roads may start at two different network ends, but the second cannot piggyback on the first (they are placed simultaneously).
- **Invention** (6th Ed; 5th Ed card was "Year of Plenty") — take any 2 resource cards from the supply (same type or two different).
Play limit: one dev card per turn, never the one bought this turn (VP cards are the exception — they're revealed for counting, not "played").

## Longest Route (6th Ed) / Longest Road (5th Ed)
First to have a continuous route of ≥5 road segments takes the tile (2 VP). "Continuous" = unbroken; an opponent's legal settlement at a choke intersection SPOKES it into separate segments. If your route is broken below the requirement, or someone builds longer, the tile moves to the new longest holder — or back to the supply if nobody has ≥5. Equal length: current holder keeps it.

## Winning
10+ VP during YOUR turn → declare immediately; reveal VP cards (including ones built this turn) to prove the count. You cannot win on another player's turn. If you forget to declare, you wait until your next turn (tournament rule 3.0.17).

## 5th vs 6th edition differences
| Aspect | 5th Ed (2015) | 6th Ed (Apr 2025) |
|---|---|---|
| Bonus tiles | Longest Road, Largest Army (Soldiers) | Longest **Route**, Largest Army (**Knights**) |
| Resource 2-card | Year of Plenty (take 2 from bank) | **Invention** (take 2 from supply) |
| Setup | Variable standard; fixed map only "for beginners" | **Fixed Setup** primary + Variable Setup |
| Components | harbor wedges on frame | refreshed art; ports printed on frame |
| VP/costs/math | identical | identical |
