# Glossary & FAQ (reference)

## Glossary
- **Almanac** — the A–Z rules encyclopedia in the 5th Ed rulebook (and the model for "where the rule actually lives").
- **Bank / supply** — the off-board card stacks; "maritime trade" is trading with it.
- **Barbarians** — C&K fleet (and T&B path-occupiers); "conquered" hexes stop producing.
- **City** — upgraded settlement; 2 VP, double production. **Metropolis** — C&K 4-VP city.
- **Commodities** — C&K secondary resources (paper/cloth/coins) produced only by cities.
- **Dev(elopment) cards** — hidden one-per-turn cards (2025 6th Ed set: Knight, VP, Monopoly, Road Building, Invention).
- **Distance Rule** — no two buildings may sit within 2 edges of each other (the corner/intersection "keep-out"). Applies during initial placement too.
- **Event die** — C&K third die: ships advance barbarians; city-gate faces grant progress cards.
- **Hex** — terrain tile; **frame** — sea border pieces; **chip/disc** — the number token.
- **Longest Road / Longest Route / Longest Trade Route** — the 2-VP bonus tile (Route = 6th Ed name; Trade Route = Seafarers version counting ships + roads).
- **Largest Army** — 2-VP tile for playing 3+ knights.
- **Pips** — dots under number tokens = dice combinations (6 pips = 16.7%).
- **Port/harbor** — coastal exchange point (3:1 generic, 2:1 resource).
- **Production roll** — 2d6 at the start of your turn.
- **Progress cards** — C&K replacement for dev cards (5 branches tied to commodity tracks).
- **Robber** — grey figure disabling one hex and enabling theft on every rolled 7.
- **Roads** — edges only, 0 VP; 15 max per color; enable Longest Route and settlement placement.
- **Snake / snake draft** — the reverse-order second starting-placement round.
- **VP** — victory point; race to 10 (base), 13 (C&K).
- **Wheat/grain, wood/lumber, brick, ore, wool/sheep** — the same resources under old/new translations; older US printings said "wolves" for sheep. 6th Ed uses wheat, wood, brick, ore, wool.

## Most-argued rulings (FAQ)
- **Can I trade when it's not my turn?** Yes — only to accept/counter with the ACTIVE player. You may even propose off-turn in casual play (tournament 3.0.7 allows off-turn offers).
- **Do dev cards count for the 7-discard?** No. Hidden dev cards are exempt and unrobtable.
- **Can the robber sit on the desert?** Yes (must, at setup). On a rolled 7 you must move it OFF its current hex — desert-to-numbered or numbered-to-desert both fine; you cannot leave it in place.
- **Can I steal from nobody?** If no opponent (and no you) has a building on the hex the robber lands on, there's no theft.
- **Can the robbed player pick which card to give?** No — the robber draws one at random, face down.
- **Can a settlement be built without a road?** Never (except initial placement). **Can a road pass through an enemy building?** No — the far-side edge is illegal. **Can your own road be "broken" by your own settlement?** It stops continuity for Longest Route counting only.
- **Longest Road with exactly 5 vs. 4?** First to ≥5 gets it; a 4-road holder gets nothing; tile returns to supply if everyone drops below 5.
- **Can I play 2 dev cards in a turn?** No — one per turn (and not a VP card "played" — VP cards are revealed for counting). Knight then a 7-roll robbery = both allowed (the 7 is production, not a dev play).
- **Road Building: must the 2 roads be adjacent?** They must each legally connect to your existing network, possibly at two different ends; one can set up the other only if the game text allows (they're built simultaneously, so the second cannot piggyback on the first).
- **Bank empty but only one player would collect?** That player takes the remainder (6th Ed rule; 5th Ed: nobody gets any — check your edition).
- **Can I win on someone else's turn?** No. If you reach 10 via their trade/robber effect, nothing happens until your turn.
- **Monopoly vs. robber?** Monopoly collects before any robber action; it doesn't move the robber.
- **Can the robber land on a hex where I have no buildings but a road does?** No — roads don't make you robable; only settlements/cities do (C&K: knights/roads? — only buildings).
- **3-player specifics:** unused color's starting corner intersections/edges are off-limits for placement; everything else per base rules.
- **5–6 player building phase:** anyone may BUILD (not trade) at the end of every turn — that's the extension's only rule change.
- **Initial resources:** from your SECOND settlement's adjacent hexes; you must keep them hidden; if the bank can't pay 2 different cards (impossible at setup) house-rule.

## Where rules actually live
Precedence (per 5th Ed Almanac note): the current published rulebook PDF takes precedence over older printings/magazines; official combo PDFs govern expansions; tournament rules (catanevents.com) override casual interpretations at events. If a user asks "is this house rule official?" — check the Almanac index entries before conceding.
