# Organized Play & Tournament Rules (reference)

Source: catanevents.com "Tournament Rules" (CATAN Studio Championship Program, revised July 2025 / 2026 restructure; numbers below are the site's own rule numbers) + Wikipedia tournament sections.

## Format
- Base game only, current English rules (6th Ed recommended; older editions allowed if consistent). Official tournament language: English.
- Minimum 12 competitors for valid results. Stages: **Prelims → (Semifinals) → Final**.
- Round structure by size (1.1.5):
  - **12–28 players:** 3 prelim games → 4-player Final (no semis).
  - **29–79:** 3 prelims → top 16 to Semifinals (serpentine seating) → semifinal winners to Final.
  - **80+:** 4 prelims; players with zero wins after Game 3 are eliminated; serpentine reseating for Game 4; top 16 → Semis → Final.
- Serpentine example (1.1.10): T1 = ranks 1,8,9,16 · T2 = 2,7,10,15 · T3 = 3,6,11,14 · T4 = 4,5,12,13.
- 3-player tables arise when (remaining players mod 4) ∈ {1,2,3} — 1.1.7 gives the exact split (e.g. 45 remaining → 9 four-player + 3 three-player tables).
- Tiebreak order for seeding: wins → total points scored → quality of victory (your points ÷ total table points per game).
- Multiple-Prelim variant (1.2): store/convention series across dates; play any number; best single prelim score counts; top 16 overall → semis/finals.

## Timing (2026 definitions)
- New board layout each round, organizer-generated (Tournament Map Library PDF at catanstudio.com); all tables play the same layout per game.
- **Map study:** 5 min global timer, discussion allowed, may end early by consensus (1.1.8).
- **Timed placements** (1.1.9): 2:00 per placement; 3:00 for the round-2 double placement; special cadence for 3-player tables (sit per announced windows).
- Seating/color/play-order picks in rank order within the timed window; play-order selection itself gets a 2-min global timer during study.
- **Turn timers** (1.1.11): official template — 90 s initial turn + 10-min bank (sharedgametimer.com template, bit.ly/catangametimer); or judge discretion after 60 min (recommended 2-min turns). Any player may call a judge to enforce timed turns after openings.
- Games end at 10 VP declared **on your turn** (3.0.17); forgetting delays you to your next turn.

## Materials & conduct clarifications
- **Port tokens not allowed** (2.0.1).
- **Hand hidden** (2.0.3): showing resource/dev cards penalized; revealing dev cards penalized (2.0.5).
- **Collusion** prohibited (3.0.15). Chain-trade ruling (3.0.13/3.0.16): a series of legal-looking trades that nets any player a card for free is one illegal trade — judges unwind it. Off-turn trade *offers* are allowed; the active player may accept (3.0.7). "Insurance"/service trades through a third party = suspect.
- **No AI assistance/software** (2.1.5, new 2026); alcohol/intoxicant policy updated (2.1.8).
- Robber live from turn 1, may be moved to desert (3.0.18). Forgotten-robber correction depends on whether the roller took further actions (3.0.19): if not, fix and steal; if yes, robber → desert, no steal.
- Dice: roll into tray/cup; die off table or fully obscured → reroll BOTH; cocked die → balance the other on top, slides off → reroll both (3.0.20).
- Judges (1.3): explain rules, resolve disputes (final rulings), never strategy; judges/organizers may not play in events they run; unreported incidents are "normal play" — no retroactive results changes.

## Practical tournament strategy (from the format)
- 4 games in ~an afternoon ⇒ points speed > perfection: openings for early VP (second-round double placement is your engine pick).
- Everyone knows the map ⇒ blocking is real; the timed placements punish over-thinking openings.
- Trade denial is the main defense vs. a runaway; remember a non-binding future promise is legal, the free-card chain is not.
