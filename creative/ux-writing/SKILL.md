# UX Writing

Write effective microcopy for digital interfaces.

## When to use

Use when asked to:

- "write button copy"
- "fix error messages"
- "write empty state copy"
- "improve onboarding copy"
- "create tooltip text"
- "define voice and tone"

## Core principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness**: use simple words, avoid jargon.
2. **Be concise**: cut unnecessary words, one idea per sentence, front-load important info.
3. **Be helpful**: tell users what to do, provide next steps, reduce anxiety.

## Patterns

### Button labels

- Use action verbs: Save, Send, Create.
- Be specific: "Add to Cart" instead of "Submit".
- Avoid generic labels like OK, Submit, Continue, vague phrases like "Click here", and negative framing.

### Error messages

Use the formula: **What happened + Why + How to fix.**

- Be specific, avoid blame, suggest a solution, use plain language.
- Example: "Page not found. Try searching or go back to the homepage." NOT "Error 404".

### Empty states

Use the formula: **What this space is for + Why it is empty + How to fill it.**

- Example: "No results for 'xyz'. Try different keywords or check your spelling."

### Loading and progress

- Under 2 seconds: spinner only.
- 2–10 seconds: "Loading your dashboard..."
- Over 10 seconds: "Processing your video. This usually takes 1–2 minutes." with progress percentage.

### Success messages

- Be specific: "Your profile has been updated" instead of "Success!".
- Confirm the action and suggest a next step when relevant.

### Tooltips

- Explain unfamiliar UI, provide context, or show keyboard shortcuts.
- Keep under 150 characters, one concept per tooltip, never hide critical info.

### Onboarding

- Acknowledge milestones, set expectations, and point to the first action.
- Use progressive disclosure; do not explain everything at once.

## Voice and tone

- **Voice** (consistent): friendly but professional, clear and direct, helpful and supportive.
- **Tone** (varies by context):
  - Success: celebratory
  - Error: calm and helpful
  - Warning: serious and clear
  - Onboarding: encouraging and patient
  - Settings: neutral and informative

## Output format

- Provide the final microcopy.
- Include a brief note on why it works.
- Offer 2–3 alternatives when useful.
