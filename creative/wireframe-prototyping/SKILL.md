# Wireframe Prototyping

Create low- to high-fidelity wireframes and HTML prototypes to validate ideas before costly development.

## When to use

- Early concept validation.
- Stakeholder alignment.
- User testing.
- Developer handoff.
- Feature exploration and rapid iteration.

## Fidelity levels

- Low: paper, whiteboard, or Balsamiq. 30 minutes to 2 hours. Basic layout, no color, best for brainstorming.
- Medium: Figma, Sketch, or Adobe XD. 2 to 8 hours. Layout, content, and basic interactions. Best for team alignment.
- High: Figma, Framer, or web-based tools. 8+ hours. Visual design, interactions, and animations. Best for developer handoff and user testing.

## Wireframe components

- Header: logo, search, cart, account.
- Hero section: product image, price, add-to-cart, reviews.
- Product details: description, specs, size guide.
- Product images: main image and thumbnails.
- Related products: 3+ cards.
- Footer: contact, FAQ, returns, shipping.

## Prototyping tools

- Figma: medium to high fidelity, real-time collaboration, free to $30/month.
- Framer: high fidelity, advanced interactions, $12+/month.
- Adobe XD: high fidelity, full interactivity, $20/month.

## Prototype flow

1. Define screens and interactions.
2. Map flows: from → to → trigger.
3. Test with 5 representative users, unmoderated, 30 minutes each.
4. Measure completion rate, task time, error rate, and satisfaction.

## Best practices

- DO start low-fidelity and get feedback early.
- DO test with real users and iterate.
- DO use consistent grids and spacing.
- DO document interaction flows.
- DO include edge cases and mobile layouts.
- DO share for collaboration.
- DON'T jump to high-fidelity too soon.
- DON'T over-design before validation.
- DON'T ignore mobile or accessibility.
- DON'T create unrealistic flows.

## Testing plan

- Objective: validate primary flows.
- Method: unmoderated remote testing.
- Participants: 5 representative users.
- Tasks: register, create a project, invite a member, edit settings.
- Success criteria: 80%+ completion, under 5 minutes per task, SUS > 70, no critical issues.
